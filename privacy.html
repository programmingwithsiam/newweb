Privacy Policy

This policy describes information used to create and operate a CodeWithSiam account and Community experience.

1. Information we collect

For email registration, we collect your name and email address. We also process the password you choose through Firebase Authentication; CodeWithSiam does not store your plain-text password. A one-time verification code is sent to your email. The server temporarily stores a protected code record, its expiry, and failed-attempt count while registration is pending.

When you use the Community, information you choose to add to your profile or submit as posts, messages, or other content is also processed to provide those features. Technical request and security logs may include timestamps, request identifiers, and limited diagnostic information.

2. How we use information

  • To verify your email, create and secure your account, and provide sign-in.
  • To display your profile and the content you choose to share in Community features.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate service problems, and meet legal obligations.

3. Storage and protection

Account authentication is provided by Firebase Authentication. Profile and Community data are stored in Firebase services, and registration verification records are kept in a server-only Realtime Database path that is not readable or writable by website clients. Server credentials and OTP hashing secrets are configured as private environment variables, not sent to your browser. We use access controls and reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing and service providers

We use Firebase to authenticate users and store service data, and an email delivery provider to send verification codes. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Retention and account deletion

Pending registration information is removed after successful verification or when it expires or is invalidated. Account and Community data are retained while needed to provide the service. To request deletion, email mdsiamahmmedloselovestroy@gmail.com from your account email. After verifying your request, we will delete your authentication account and associated personal profile data, except records we must retain for legal, security, or operational reasons.

6. Your choices and contact

You may choose what optional profile details or Community content to provide. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact mdsiamahmmedloselovestroy@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
